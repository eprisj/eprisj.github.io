1. Independence

Editorial decisions are made by the editors. Articles are not for sale. Studios, galleries and brands cannot buy coverage, and being featured does not require any payment.

2. What we publish

Interviews, essays, studio and project features, reviews and reports on contemporary art, architecture and interior design, with their own photography or properly licensed images.

3. Interviews and questionnaires

Answers are published as supplied, edited only for length, clarity and translation. People interviewed see how they are presented and may ask for changes before publication.

4. Sponsored material and gifts

Any commercial relationship, sponsored piece or material gift relevant to an article is clearly labelled on it. We have none that we do not disclose.

5. Accuracy and corrections

We check facts, names and credits before publication. Errors are corrected promptly and, where the change is significant, noted on the article. Report an error by e-mail and we will look at it.

6. Use of AI

Translations and some drafting may be machine-assisted and are reviewed by an editor before they appear. Articles and images are not published without human editorial review, and we do not present synthetic images as photographs of real places or works.

7. Conflicts of interest

Authors and editors declare relationships with the people or companies they write about, and we reassign pieces where a conflict cannot be managed.

8. Complaints

If you think we have treated you or your work unfairly, write to the editors at the address below. We reply within a few working days.
