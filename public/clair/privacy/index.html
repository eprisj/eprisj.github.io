Clair – Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Clair is an iOS app by EPRIS Journal for finding exhibitions worldwide. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. You can use it without an account.

Without an account

Saved shows, plans, reminders, your city and settings stay on your phone. The app downloads public exhibition listings and pictures from the sources it names in Settings; those services see an ordinary request from your phone.

Location, camera, calendar, nearby phones

Each is asked for only when you use the feature. Your location is used on the phone to show what is near you and how to get there; it is not sent to us. The camera reads a poster you point it at, on the phone. Calendar access only adds the show or day you choose. Nearby uses Bluetooth and the local network to pass a show or a plan to a phone beside you, directly, without our server.

With an account (optional)

If you sign in with Apple, we keep an Apple user identifier and the name you give. Apple can hide your email; we do not ask for it. To plan with friends we store what you choose to share: friends you added, outings and plans, shows you mark as going or seen, agreements made with friends, and shows or artists you submit for moderation. Your friends see only what you share with them. You can block and report people.

Deleting your data

Delete account in the app’s menu removes your account and everything stored with it from our server at once.

Where it is kept

On a server run by EPRIS Journal. We do not sell or share it. Server logs may include IP addresses and are kept only as long as they are useful for security and troubleshooting.

Children

Clair is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests: eprisjournal@outlook.com.
