1. Ownership

Texts, photographs, illustrations and design in the journal belong to us or to credited authors, photographers, artists and studios who allowed their publication. Open-licensed material (for example from Wikimedia Commons) remains under its licence and is credited. See also the Terms of Use.

2. Credits

We aim to credit every image and text. If a credit is missing or wrong, write to us and we will correct it.

3. Reporting an infringement

If you believe a work in the journal infringes your rights, send a message to the address below containing:

  • the address (URL) of the page, or the name of the article in the app;
  • a description of the work you say is yours and how you can show it;
  • your name, your contact details and your connection to the work;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised, and that the information you give is accurate.

4. What we do

We acknowledge a notice promptly, assess it, and remove or amend the material where appropriate, normally within a few working days. We may tell the person who supplied the material that a complaint was made. Repeated or deliberately false notices may be disregarded.

5. Counter-notice

If material you supplied was removed and you believe that was a mistake, write to us with the details and we will review it.

6. Permissions

To reuse, translate or reprint our work, ask first. Short quotation with credit and a link is welcome, as described in the Terms of Use.
