EPRIS Journal · for iPhone and iPad

The journal, readable without a signal.

Every piece is typeset for reading, the photography opens at full size, and anything you save stays on the device, photographs and all. Free, with no account and no tracking.

FreeiPhone and iPadiOS 17 or later

On the iPhone

Six screens

The front page, a piece open for reading, the quizzes and the search across the whole archive.

What is inside

Built for reading, not for scrolling

01

Typeset for reading

Interviews and essays laid out for iPhone and iPad rather than squeezed from a web page. Adjustable text size on top of Dynamic Type, and a full dark mode.

02

Offline in one tap

Save a piece and the words and every photograph download to the device. Read on a plane or underground, and reclaim the storage whenever you want.

03

Photography at full size

Open any photograph full screen, pinch to zoom, save it or share it.

04

Quizzes

Most pieces end with a short quiz, some answered by picking a photograph. Every quiz is in one list, with your best scores.

05

The whole archive

Search by title, author, subject or tag, in the app or from the iPhone’s own search. The app remembers where you stopped reading.

06

No account, no tracking

No sign-up, no advertising, no analytics. What you save and how far you have read stay on your device.

On the iPad

Legal

Privacy and terms

The app collects no personal data. There is no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising; what you save, how far you have read and your settings stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

The full details, including what the app connects to in order to show the journal, are in the Privacy Policy. The rules for reading, quoting and sharing the journal are in the Terms of Use.
