Legal

Privacy policy

Updated 7 August 2026

The EPRIS Journal app for iOS (the "app") collects no personal data, stores none, and sends none. This policy sets out exactly how the app handles information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up and no accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, or any other personal data.

Tracking and advertising

The app does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, shows no advertising, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • a copy of the journal's content, so it can be read without a connection;
  • a technical marker of the content version, so unchanged content isn't downloaded twice;
  • web view data for the sections that are served from the site.

This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed completely when the app is deleted.

Network requests

To show the journal, the app contacts our servers (eprisjournal.com and api.eprisjournal.com). As with any web request, this includes an IP address and the app type — technically necessary to deliver a response. We do not use these records for profiling and do not tie them to an individual.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page along with its date.
